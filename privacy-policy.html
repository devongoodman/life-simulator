Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2025

Welcome to Life Simulator ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Life Simulator is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • No personal information: We do not collect your name, email, address, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • No account required: You can play the game without creating an account or signing in.
  • Local game data: Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Multiplayer Features

When using the multiplayer feature:

  • Player names you enter are only shared with other players in your game session.
  • Connections are made directly between players (peer-to-peer) and are not stored on any server.
  • Game session data is temporary and is deleted when the game ends.

Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using standard browser/app storage. We do not have access to this data, and it never leaves your device except during active multiplayer sessions.

Third-Party Services

Our app may use the following third-party services:

  • PeerJS: Used for multiplayer connectivity. Please refer to their privacy policy for more information.
  • Google STUN servers: Used to establish peer-to-peer connections for multiplayer games.

Children's Privacy

Life Simulator does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The game does not require any personal information to play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. Your local game data can be cleared by clearing the app's data in your device settings.
